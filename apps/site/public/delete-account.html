Delete your Merge account

Merge by jackedengineer

You can delete your Merge account and its data at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

In the app

  1. Open Merge and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

If you cannot use the app

Email trainwithchirag@gmail.com from the email address on your account, or tell us the phone number you signed up with, and ask us to delete your account. We will confirm it is you and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, date of birth, gender, pronouns, height, preferences and location.
  • Your photos and your written answers.
  • Your pull requests, merges, passes, blocks and reports.
  • Your conversations and messages, for you and for the people you talked to.
  • Your notification settings and your phone’s notification token.
  • Your sign-in details (phone number or email address).

What is kept

Nothing that identifies you is kept in the app’s database. Copies in our service providers’ routine backups and technical logs expire on those providers’ normal schedules, usually within 30 days, and are not used for anything else.

Deleting some data without deleting your account

In the Profile tab you can remove photos, change your answers and details, and hide your gender, pronouns and height. You can end a conversation by unmatching in the chat.
