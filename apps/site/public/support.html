Help and contact

Merge is made by jackedengineer. For help, questions about your data, or to report a problem, email trainwithchirag@gmail.com. We aim to reply within a few days.

Common questions

How do I report or block someone?

Use “Report or block” on their profile, on a pull request from them, or in your chat with them. Reporting also blocks them. They are not told.

How do I delete my account?

Profile tab, then Delete account. See how deletion works.

I am not getting a sign-in code

Text message codes are not available in every country yet. Choose “Use email instead” on the sign-in screen.

Who can see my location?

Other members see your area and a rough distance. Your exact position is never stored or shown. See the privacy policy.
